# Blueprint — Related Work (OWAD)

### Paragraph 1 — Neuroseismo / precursors
- Role: theme opening + boundary
- Main claim: Precursor and animal-response literature motivates biosignal monitoring near events but stops short of operational forecasting and of a timed onset estimand on continuous physiology.
- Evidence IDs: S01–S07, S04
- Forbidden: operational forecast endorsement

### Paragraph 2 — REM as biosignal
- Role: theme synthesis + limitation
- Main claim: Stress alters rodent REM/sleep, and continuous staging makes REM series measurable, yet REM remains an indirect marker and classical site reports used profile range rather than changepoint onset.
- Evidence IDs: S08–S13, S18
- Forbidden: equating REM with measured stress

### Paragraph 3 — Changepoint / regime-shift methods
- Role: method family
- Main claim: Time-series changepoint surveys and Bayesian changepoint formulations address regime shifts; short heterogeneous windows need explicit uncertainty and careful model comparison rather than high-capacity detectors alone.
- Evidence IDs: S20, S21, S19, S14–S17
- Forbidden: claiming novelty of changepoint theory itself

### Paragraph 3b — Open-world anomaly detection (venue fit)
- Role: OWAD theme + critical boundary
- Main claim: Open-world / drifting AD literature (surveys + normality-shift / lifelong AD) frames anomalies under evolving normality and scarce labels; this paper is a small-n offline regime-onset case in an irregular seismic context, not a continual adaptation system.
- Evidence IDs: S22–S25
- Forbidden: claiming we detect normality shift online, adapt detectors, or solve continual AD

### Paragraph 4 — Positioning bridge
- Role: bridge to this paper
- Main claim: We position a single Bayesian changepoint \(\tau\) on eight-day event-aligned REM **mean** windows with LOO screening and \(\mathbb{E}[\tau]\) stability, under explicit non-prediction / non-stress claims.
- Evidence IDs: S17, S04
- Forbidden: contribution bullet list; long roadmap; Multivariate as primary object
