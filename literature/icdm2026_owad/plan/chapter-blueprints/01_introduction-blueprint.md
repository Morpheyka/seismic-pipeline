### Paragraph 1
- Role: context + motivation boundary
- Main claim: Incomplete EQ-preparation physics and many precursor observations frame the domain; this paper asks whether a timed regime-onset anomaly exists in physiological series near a seismic event (not an operational forecast).
- Evidence IDs: S01, S02, S03, S04
- Contrast or transition: from geophysics to animal/biosignal routes

### Paragraph 2
- Role: biological route

- Evidence IDs: S05, S06, S07

### Paragraph 3
- Role: method landscape + limitation
- Main claim: Stress alters rodent sleep including REM, but stressor-specific patterns make REM only an indirect state marker.
- Evidence IDs: S08, S09, S10, S11
- Contrast or transition: to site measurement and estimand choice

### Paragraph 4
- Role: gap / estimand contrast + OWAD task statement
- Main claim: Continuous hypnograms yield event-aligned REM mean summaries around events; classical site reports tracked profile range (~2–4 day effect window), whereas we estimate regime-onset changepoint \(\tau\) on an 8-day window. Open-world framing: irregular seismic context and scarce labels, not a continual AD system.
- Evidence IDs: S12, S12b, S13, S18, S22–S25 (light touch in Intro; depth in RW)
- Contrast or transition: to statistical bottlenecks
- Forbidden content: long \(\tau\)-index inversion digression; claiming lifelong AD

### Paragraph 5
- Role: bottleneck cascade
- Main claim: Tiny heterogeneous samples and autocorrelation rule out high-capacity black boxes and naive CV; Bayesian single-changepoint inference with posterior uncertainty fits the data regime.
- Evidence IDs: S14, S15, S16, S19
- Contrast or transition: to contributions
- Forbidden content: promising multi-changepoint hierarchy as already done

### Paragraph 6

- Evidence IDs: S17, S04
